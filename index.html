Profile

Hardware that has to work the first time.

I'm an embedded hardware engineer working across schematic design, PCB layout and board bring-up for STM32, ESP32 and LoRa-based systems. My work spans validating analog and digital circuits, interfacing peripherals over SPI, I2C, UART and ADC, and debugging boards with a DSO, logic analyzer and multimeter until every signal checks out. Most recently that's meant multilayer PCBs — schematic through Gerbers — for autonomous ground vehicles built for defense and automation deployment, with EMI/EMC and reliability considered from the first layout pass.
